Cue — your cue to speak
by Digitalgrub

BEFORE YOU INSTALL — please note:

• Cue runs on Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit).

• Cue needs Ollama, the free local AI engine, which is installed separately:
     1. Download and install Ollama from  https://ollama.com
     2. Open it once (it runs in your system tray)
     3. Pull a small model, e.g.:   ollama pull llama3.2:1b

  Without Ollama, capture and the knowledge base still work, but the AI
  brief / questions / "what to say" features need it (or an Anthropic API
  key for Claude, which you can add in Settings).

• This installer includes everything:
     - Teams desktop capture
     - "Pick a window" capture (any window with real caption/subtitle text)
     - System audio capture, transcribed locally
     - Transcribe an audio or video file you already have
     - Knowledge base, running brief, questions, and "what you could say"
  Speech models (a few hundred MB) download the first time you use them.

• All your data stays on your machine. No telemetry, no account. If you choose
  to turn on a cloud AI engine in Settings, your meeting text is sent to that
  provider; that is off by default. See PRIVACY.md.

• IMPORTANT: Cue transcribes everyone in the meeting, not just you. In many
  places, recording or transcribing people without their consent is unlawful
  or against employer policy. Tell the other participants and get their
  agreement before you capture.
